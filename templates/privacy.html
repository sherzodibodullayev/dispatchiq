Privacy Policy

How information collected through this website and the AI copilot on it is handled.

Scope. This policy covers this website only. DispatchIQ is at the planning stage and there is no product in operation, so there is no customer fleet data, driver data, ELD data or document processing happening anywhere behind this site. When a product exists, it will have its own data-processing terms in a signed agreement, and those will govern operational data rather than this page.

Who is responsible

DispatchIQ is a working product name for a venture founded by {{ founder.name }}. No U.S. legal entity has been formed. Until one exists, the founder is the point of contact for any question about information submitted here: {{ company.email }}.

What is collected

Messages you send the copilot

The assistant on this site sends your messages to a commercial AI model provider in order to produce a reply. Conversations are stored against a randomly generated conversation identifier so the assistant can follow the thread, and are retained for up to seven days before expiring. There is no account, and nothing ties a conversation to you unless you type something identifying into it.

Do not paste driver records, customer contracts, load data, or anything else you would not put in an email to a stranger. The assistant is instructed not to repeat third-party personal data back, but the sensible protection is not sending it.

Voice messages

If you use the microphone, the recording is uploaded, transcribed, and the audio file is deleted immediately afterwards. The transcript is then treated exactly like a typed message.

Anything you volunteer

If you give the assistant a name, an email address or details about your operation so the founder can follow up, that is kept for the purpose you gave it for and nothing else. The same applies to the newsletter form in the footer.

Technical information

The server records ordinary request logs and applies rate limiting by IP address to stop abuse of the assistant. There is no third-party advertising, no tracking pixel and no cross-site profiling on this site.

Who it is shared with

The AI model provider that generates the assistant's replies, and the hosting and caching infrastructure that runs the site. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention

Conversations expire after seven days. Audio is deleted as soon as it has been transcribed. Anything you sent deliberately so the founder could reply — a name and an email — is kept until it is no longer needed for that, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

Your choices

The Clear button under the composer drops the conversation and starts a new one. To ask what is held about you, or to have it deleted, email {{ company.email }}. Depending on where you live you may have statutory rights of access, correction, deletion and portability; requests are honoured regardless of whether a statute compels it.

Children

This site is aimed at trucking businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially — particularly once a product exists and processes operational data — the change will be posted here before it takes effect.

This page describes practice, not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for the data agreement that will accompany the product.
